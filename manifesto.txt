3.  GROUP  STRUCTURE  
We  operate  using  two  distinct  channels  to  keep  things  organized.  
🟢  Group  A:  GreenLight  (The  Signal  Channel)  
●  Purpose:  Verified  signals  and  confirmed  opportunities  only.  ●  Action:  When  a  notification  pops  up  here,  it  is  time  to  move.  ●  Rules:  No  chatting.  No  spam.  Strictly  business.

4.  THE  MONEY  RULES  (The  Finder’s  Tax)  
This  is  the  engine  that  keeps  ApeX  running.  
The  Rule:  If  a  member  brings  an  opportunity/signal,  they  have  the  exclusive  right  to  choose  whether  to  
charge
 
a
 
tax
 
or
 
share
 
it
 
for
 
free.
 ●  Tax  Applied:  By  default,  you  must  pay  a  Finder’s  Tax  of
 (or  agreed  percentage)  of  your  net  profit  to  that  member.  ●  Tax  Waived  (Opt-Out):  The  finder  can  explicitly  state  "No  Tax"  when  posting.  In  this  case,  
you
 
keep
 
100%
 
of
 
your
 
profits.
 ●  Default  Status:  If  the  finder  does  not  specify,  the  tax  is  assumed  to  be  MANDATORY .  The  "Rogue  Play"  Clause:  Sometimes,  the  group  might  analyze  a  signal  and  decide  it  is  "Too  Risky"  or  "Not  Approved"  for  
the
 
official
 
GreenLight
 
channel.
 ●  If  you  decide  to  take  that  risk  on  your  own  anyway,  and  you  LOSE  money,  that  is  on  you.  ●  However,  if  you  decide  to  take  the  risk  and  you  WIN  (make  profit),  you  still  owe  the  
Finder’s
 
Tax
 
(unless
 
it
 
was
 
explicitly
 
waived
 
by
 
the
 
finder).
 ○  Why?  Because  without  that  member  bringing  the  idea,  you  would  not  have  seen  it  at  
all.
 
We
 
honor
 
the
 
source.
 Failure  to  Pay:  
Anyone  caught  making  money  from  the  group's  info  without  paying  the  Finder’s  Tax  (when  
applicable)
 
will
 
be
 
banned
 
immediately
 
and
 
permanently.
 

5.  MODE  OF  OPERATION  The  Daily  Grind  (Monday  -  Friday)  
●  Scout:  Every  member  is  expected  to  look  for  opportunities  online  (X/Twitter,  Telegram,  
Niche
 
Forums).
 ●  Share:  Post  your  findings  in  Base  using  the  Submission  Format.  ●  Vet:  The  group  analyzes  the  play.  Is  the  liquidity  locked?  Is  the  developer  safe?  ●  Execute:  If  approved,  it  moves  to  GreenLight .  
The  Weekly  Sync  (Saturday)  
●  The  Space:  We  hold  a  voice  call/space  once  a  week.  ●  Agenda:  Review  the  week's  wins/losses  and  plan  for  the  next  week.  
The  Monthly  Audit  (End  of  Month)  
●  PnL  Review:  We  look  at  how  much  we  made  collectively.  ●  The  Purge:  We  review  member  activity.  ○  Did  you  contribute?  ○  Were  you  active?  ○  If  the  answer  is  "No,"  you  are  removed  to  make  space  for  someone  hungry.
